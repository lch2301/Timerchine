Online audio support — unmodified standalone tools invoked as separate processes.
Online tools are bundled only when the EnableExternalLinks build option is true.
No browser cookies, account passwords, or authentication tokens are collected.
External links require the user's confirmation of download/alarm-use rights
in the app settings. The saved acknowledgement applies to all timer dialogs.
Before downloading, Timerchine checks extractor metadata for access restrictions
and DRM indicators and pins a single direct HTTP/HTTPS file. Proxy/geo bypass,
encrypted/fragmented manifests, and force-playing DRM formats are not enabled.
The YouTube signature/n JavaScript challenge processing remains available.
These restrictions do not establish that any particular use is lawful or that
the challenge processing falls outside statutory anti-circumvention rules.
